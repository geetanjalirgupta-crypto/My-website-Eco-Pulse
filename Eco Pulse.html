Calculation Formula, Emission Factors & Documentation

ISO-14064 Compliant
Total estimated emissions (kg CO₂e) = Distance (km) × Emission factor (g CO₂e/passenger-km) ÷ 1000

Each emission factor represents lifecycle or operational grams of carbon dioxide equivalent per passenger-kilometer (g CO₂e/passenger-km), adjusted by occupancy where applicable.

Petrol car 190 g/pkm

Standard ICE petrol passenger sedan. Single occupant baseline, scalable by carpool occupancy.

Diesel car 170 g/pkm

Compression-ignition diesel engine with higher fuel economy, higher NOx/tailpipe soot.

Electric car 50 g/pkm

Well-to-wheel generation estimate based on regional electrical grid generation composition.

Motorcycle 100 g/pkm

4-stroke commuter motorcycle / petrol scooter with single rider operational load.

Public Transit Bus 40 g/pkm

Municipal transit bus based on standard average transit occupancy of 25–30 passengers.

Suburban Rail / Metro 30 g/pkm

Electric commuter rail / metro with high commuter load factor and regenerative braking.

Bicycle (Human-Powered) 0 g/pkm

Active mobility with zero direct operational tailpipe greenhouse gas emissions.

Note on Estimation Accuracy: These calculations are verified scientific estimates and are not presented as exact point measurements. Actual emissions generated will vary depending on vehicle curb weight, driving speed, severe traffic congestion, auxiliary air conditioning load, terrain elevation profile, local electricity grid generation mix, and exact passenger loading.
